Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

1. Overview

Andheri Haqeeqat Uploader is a personal video upload tool operated by the Andheri Haqeeqat team ("we," "our"). This policy explains how data is handled by this application.

2. Data Collection

This app does not collect, store, or process any personal data from third-party users. The app is used exclusively by the channel owner to upload video content to TikTok.

3. TikTok Account Data

The app accesses the following TikTok account data through OAuth authorization:

- TikTok Open ID (for authentication)
- Video upload permissions (to publish content)

This data is stored locally on the operator's device and is never shared with third parties.

4. Video Content

Videos uploaded through this app are stored locally on the operator's device before upload. Once uploaded, the videos are hosted on TikTok's platform and subject to TikTok's Privacy Policy.

5. Third-Party Services

This app uses TikTok's Content Posting API. TikTok's privacy practices are governed by their own Privacy Policy at https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy.

6. Data Security

Authentication tokens are stored locally on the operator's device. No data is transmitted to any server other than TikTok's official API endpoints.

7. No Third-Party Users

This application has no third-party users. It is a personal tool used by a single individual to manage their own TikTok channel.
